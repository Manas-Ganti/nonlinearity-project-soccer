## E. Spells and score state: is excitation conditional?

> Reading. Spells do not amplify excitation: eta_in is never significant and the in-minus-out contrast is centred on zero on every source. What a spell does is shift the *rate*: a team in a spell shoots about 4-9% more and its opponent about 11% less, and leaving that out of the background (`eta_in without gamma`) manufactures an apparent spell effect in the kernel. Score state is different: eta_trail and eta_lead reject after Holm on all three arms while eta_level is essentially zero. But every fit puts tau at or next to the 8-minute edge of its box and the likelihood keeps improving out to 30 minutes, which is the signature of a slow trend the background does not carry, not of chances triggering chances within minutes. The obvious candidate is a trailing team's pressure rising as time runs out, which a time-constant score term cannot absorb. The bootstrap tests the exact fitted Poisson null, so it rejects on any such misfit; a rejection here says the null is incomplete, not that momentum exists. Full StatsBomb has null critical values about 100x smaller than the other arms and twice their estimation bias, for reasons not yet understood; the no-one-club arm is the cleaner StatsBomb read.
